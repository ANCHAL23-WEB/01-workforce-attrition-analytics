# Business Problem

Employees on the bench are at higher risk of attrition, and losing high-value employees costs the company significantly — in idle salary, lost productivity, and replacement cost.

This project analyzes HR, bench allocation, and finance data together to:
1. Identify which employees are at high attrition risk using bench time and other predictors
2. Quantify the financial cost of bench time and attrition
3. Rank employees by potential business impact if lost — so leadership can prioritize retention efforts where they matter most
